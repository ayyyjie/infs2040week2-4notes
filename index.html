Week 2

Double Diamond & Ideation

The base tier holds up everything else: how projects run, and the CX/UX/UI vocabulary you'll reuse all exam.

Worth remembering

  • Double Diamond = Discover → Define → Develop → Deliver, two diverge/converge cycles.
  • Waterfall: ever-increasing divergence between original idea and final product. Agile: small scope, small divergences, iterative correction.
  • Customer Journey Map tracks touchpoints, sentiment, needs, frustrations and improvement opportunities across stages.
  • Case study line to remember: the honey campaign found its positioning in "it all starts with a bee."

Week 3

Personas & Ideation

The middle tier: how a project actually starts, who you're designing for, and why plans quietly fail.

Worth remembering

  • Concept statement: 100–150 words, a mission statement readable by any audience, iterated not written once.
  • An authentic persona is research-based, has goals, applies to exactly one user role — never a demographic caricature.
  • Planning fallacy (Kahneman & Tversky, 1979): we underrate our own plan's time/cost/risk — fix is the outside view.
  • Pre-mortem (Gary Klein, 2007): imagine the project has already failed, then explain why — sharper, more specific risks than a normal brainstorm.
  • Synthetic AI research caveat: "Claude is guessing plausibly, not measuring — treat every number as a hypothesis."

Week 4

Project Scope

The top tier: the formal documents and processes that keep scope from creeping.

Worth remembering

  • Scope management's six processes: define scope management → collect requirements → define scope → create SOW → validate scope → control scope.
  • SOW vs. Scope: the SOW is the high-level document (mission, deliverables, success criteria); scope is one element inside it — scope has no milestones.
  • SOW vs. Project Charter: SOW doesn't authorise budget or resources — the charter does.
  • Framestore case: UI = the VFX shots, UX = how a project runs through the facility, CX = the bespoke design of the client's project and pipeline.
